# Lumen 1.0.7

Your selected wallpaper now stays after you quit Lumen. Imported images become the macOS desktop image. Playing videos and procedural scenes continue through a small background helper, with a still image underneath as a fallback. The helper exits when you reopen Lumen.

Settings now includes **Keep Animating After Quit**. Turn it off to leave a still image instead. **Launch at Login** can restart Lumen after your next sign-in.

The macOS Video Wallpaper catalog keeps one reusable entry labeled **Lumen** when you switch videos or enable the feature again.

Requires macOS 14 or newer. Download `Lumen-1.0.7.dmg`, open it, and drag Lumen to Applications.

This DMG is ad-hoc signed because no Developer ID certificate is configured for this release. macOS may warn that it cannot verify the developer. After confirming the download is from this repository, Control-click Lumen in Applications and choose **Open**, or use **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway** after a blocked launch.
